INFO     PARTLY_CALLABLE_BINS
  This input records which bins are called (at least 10% of their 100 bases callable, fq2psmcfa's
  rule) but not how many of their bases are, as psmcfa does, so the fit treats every called bin as
  100 bases, as C psmc does. 3.8% of bins are missing (N), and 191,470 called bins (1.0% of them)
  border those runs: they hold fewer than 100 callable bases unless a run starts or ends at a bin
  boundary. If missing data are also scattered in runs shorter than a bin, most called bins do,
  which this input cannot show. Partly callable bins show fewer heterozygous sites, which biases
  theta, the times and Ne low by about their missing share.
  -> To measure it, run `psmc-rs qc` on the data the psmcfa was made from: a VCF with its callable
     mask (--vcf-mask) or an all-sites VCF from `bcftools call -c`. It reports the share of callable
     bases of the called bins, and a fit of that VCF weighs each bin by its callable bases
     (--bin-callable auto), which removes the bias.
